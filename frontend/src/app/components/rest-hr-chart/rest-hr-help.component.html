Heart rate awake at rest

Your heart rate while awake and not moving, so one day compares with another.

  • Counted: stretches of 15+ minutes with no steps, outside sleep. The first 5 minutes of each are skipped while you settle.
  • Each 5-minute block gives one median. The line is the day's median of those blocks.
  • Bands: the 25th–75th percentile of the blocks, and fainter, the 5th–95th.
  • Sensor spikes over 25 bpm from the readings around them are dropped.
  • A gap is a day that could not be measured: no sleep record, or no still stretch long enough.
